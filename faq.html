What is Loqelo?

Loqelo is a planned marketplace for connecting people and businesses with service professionals for home, garden and office needs.

When will Loqelo launch?

Loqelo is planning to launch in February 2027. The exact launch day has not been announced.

Is Loqelo available worldwide?

Loqelo is collecting interest from multiple countries. The countries and services available at launch have not yet been confirmed.

How does the process work?

Describe your need, receive relevant matches, compare offers and choose the provider that suits you. This is the intended platform journey, not a live booking flow yet.

How can I join as a customer?

Use the customer waitlist form, add your email and country of residence, then agree to receive Loqelo launch updates.

How can I join as a service provider?

Choose the provider waitlist, provide your email and country of operation, and consent to launch information. This does not create a live provider profile.

Will I need to pay to join the waitlist?

The waitlist does not require a payment. Pricing and availability for services after launch have not been announced.

What happens to my details?

The information you submit is used for the pre-launch contact list and updates based on your consent. Read the Privacy Policy draft, which must be approved by Loqelo before public deployment.
